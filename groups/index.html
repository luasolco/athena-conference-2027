Good to know

Questions, answered.

Which tickets qualify for group rates?

General Admission and VIP. Student tickets ($145 high school, $295 college) and ATHENA Awards Celebration day only tickets stay at the published rate and are not eligible.

Can I mix General Admission and VIP in one group?

Yes. Contact the ATHENA team with the number of each ticket and we'll set up your group.

Can we combine the group rate with other codes?

No. One code per order, and discounts do not stack. Your team gets the best single rate available.

What if someone on the team can't attend?

All ticket sales are final, but substitutions may be permitted. Contact the ATHENA team with the new attendee's name.

Is the hotel included?

No. ATHENA has a discounted rate at the Hilton Los Angeles/Universal City: $239 + tax per night, bookable from January 25 to February 2, 2027.

How does checkout work?

ATHENA uses Zeffy for secure, no-fee registration. Zeffy may add an optional tip at checkout. To remove it, select Custom Tip and enter $0.
