Good to know

Questions, answered.

Who qualifies for this offer?

Anyone who has received an ATHENA Award from a community or organization that presents ATHENA Awards, in any year. The offer is one guest per ATHENA Award Recipient.

Who can I bring?

Anyone who has not registered for ATHENA 2027 yet. We especially hope you'll bring someone who is new to ATHENA.

I already bought my ticket. Can I still add a guest?

Yes. Contact the ATHENA team before the offer ends and we'll add your guest.

When does the offer end?

December 15, 2026. VIP seats may sell out sooner.

Can I combine it with other discounts?

No. One offer or code per order, and discounts do not stack.

Is the hotel included?

No. ATHENA has a discounted rate at the Hilton Los Angeles/Universal City: $239 + tax per night, bookable from January 25 to February 2, 2027.

How does checkout work?

ATHENA uses Zeffy for secure, no-fee registration. Zeffy may add an optional tip at checkout. To remove it, select Custom Tip and enter $0.
