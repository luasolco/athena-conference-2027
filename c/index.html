More Than a Conference

Find your people. Expand your influence. Lead what's next.

The 2027 ATHENA Conference & Awards Celebration brings together women leaders, allies, entrepreneurs, executives, and changemakers for three days of learning, recognition, and authentic connection. Experience inspiring keynotes, interactive workshops, wellness sessions, and a global community committed to creating impact that lasts far beyond the event.

Who we serve

Trusted by leaders at 500+ organizations, including

From Fortune 500 to startups, plus countless entrepreneurs and small teams, leaders choose ATHENA International to grow together.

Who this is for

Wherever you are in your leadership, it's time to plan what's next.

Whether you're stepping into leadership, leading a team through change, growing a business, or deciding what comes next, ATHENA 2027 gives you the room, the tools, and the people to do it well.

01

The emerging leader

You're just getting started. You stepped into your first lead role, or you're about to. You want to build influence before the title catches up, speak with confidence in rooms where you're the newest voice, and grow a network of mentors and peers who will vouch for you.

You leave withInfluence at any level and the start of your personal board.

02

The advancing leader

You're ready to grow your influence beyond your role. You lead a team inside an organization, from a regional business to a global enterprise, and you're expected to deliver through reorgs, AI adoption, and budget cuts. You need frameworks that work in the real world and peers who understand the pressure.

You leave withTools for leading under pressure and a plan to stay visible and valuable.

03

The leader in transition

You're entering a new chapter. You've achieved a lot, and you can feel there's more you're meant to do. Maybe you left, were cut, or know a move is coming. Whether what's next is a new role or your own business, you want clarity, courage, and people who have made the same leap.

You leave withA clear next step and a circle that holds you to it.

Also in the room: executive corporate leaders, founders and small business owners, nonprofit and community leaders, educators, ATHENA Award Recipients, ATHENA Certified Facilitators, students from high school to college, and the allies who champion women's leadership.

You don't need a title to belong here. Join our community of human-centered leaders.

The conversations that never happen on a call.

The part ATHENA 2025 Conference attendees asked for most: more time to actually talk. So both evenings end around a small table.

  1. Pick your table. After you register, choose the speaker you want to have dinner with in the ATHENA Conference Hub on the Mighty Networks app (iOS/Android/Web). First come, first served.
  2. Walk over together. Restaurants a short walk up the hill from the hotel, with hotel carts available, plus a table at the Hilton for anyone who prefers to stay close.

Hands-On Micro-Labs

Skills you can use on Monday.

Leadership is not built by watching. It is built by doing. Interactive micro-labs give you room for real application, peer learning, and skills you can put to work right away: professional presence in an AI-driven world, stress you can manage, productivity you can protect, career growth you can steer, and teams you can lead through uncertainty.

Networking That Opens Doors

Relationships that accelerate opportunity.

Some of the most valuable conversations happen between sessions. ATHENA International's intentional networking connects you with leaders, entrepreneurs, artists, nonprofit innovators, and change-makers from around the world.

The Future of Leadership

Keynotes and panels on AI, change, and influence.

Forget passive listening. Keynote speakers and panelists challenge assumptions, take on the leadership questions that matter right now, and start conversations that keep going long after the session ends. Human-centered AI, influence at any level, resilience under pressure, workplace wellbeing, and the future of leadership in a world that will not slow down.

Wellness & Renewal

Recharge and reflect.

Great leaders need more than professional development. They need space to recharge. Morning wellness experiences run throughout the three days: guided sessions, time to reflect, and restorative practices that keep you energized, present, and focused. You leave renewed, not depleted.

The moment this is built for

You've built a career, a business, a network, and a life of impact. Now you're asking: what's next?

I'm an artist, executive, technologist, and Brazilian immigrant. After more than a decade working in AI and innovation, including seven years in AI Cloud partnerships at Microsoft, I discovered the transformative power of nonprofit communities through the Microsoft Change Agent program.

ATHENA International and The WIT Network Emerging Leaders Program challenged me to dream bigger, lead with even greater courage, and embrace a simple truth: leadership isn't about fitting in. It's about being fully yourself and paving a path for others to do the same.

The ATHENA community helped me navigate the journey from corporate leadership to entrepreneurship, motherhood, and founding LUASOL Collective, without having to choose between being a leader and being an artist.

Today, I believe that the way we love ourselves shapes how we belong. And belonging is one of the most powerful forces for change. When we know our worth, we lead more boldly, build stronger communities, and create greater impact together.

I'm returning to ATHENA 2027 as an ATHENA Board Director & Leadership Institute Facilitator because this community continues to help me grow with greater clarity, confidence, and purpose.

I hope you'll join us in Los Angeles as we step into our next chapter together.
